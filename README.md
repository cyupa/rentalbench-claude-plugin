# RentalBench

![RentalBench logo](assets/logo.png)

Connect an existing RentalBench staff account to check rental availability,
manage customers and orders, and work across your locations in Claude.
RentalBench for Claude is provided by Applicodo SRL. Your staff role, location
memberships and plan determine what you can see and change.

## Connect

Open RentalBench in Claude and sign in on RentalBench's website when prompted.
Review the staff account, workspace, locations and permissions on the consent
screen. To disconnect, remove RentalBench in Claude's connector settings or
revoke the Claude session in RentalBench account settings.

## Try it

- Show today's pickups and returns at my Central location.
- Check availability for the camera kit at Central from Friday to Monday.
- Help me reserve a camera kit for an existing customer and preview the rental agreement.

Upload spreadsheets and images in RentalBench. Claude can work with existing
inventory imports after upload. Complete documents, payment actions,
credentials and customer signatures use authenticated RentalBench pages or
secure customer links.

## Data and support

The skill guides rental workflows. Its tools call the RentalBench MCP server at
`https://app.rentalbench.com/mcp/claude`. RentalBench receives tool inputs and
returns records within your staff permissions, including customer and staff
contact details when you request them. Writes can update rental records, send a
document to a specified customer or publish changes to your webstore.
